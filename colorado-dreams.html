Must Hike Before 2027

  • Lake Agnes Trail - State Forest State Park
  • Lower, Middle and Upper Blue Lakes Trail - Mt Sneffels Wilderness
  • Silver Dollar Lake and Murray Lake Trail - Arapaho National Forest
  • Island Lake and Ice Lake via Ice Lakes Trail - San Juan National Forest
  • Maroon Bells Scenic Loop Trail - Maroon Bells Snowmass Wilderness

Hike Difficulty Rankings

  1. Maroon Bells Scenic Loop Trail - Easy - 2.0mi
  2. Lake Agnes Trail - Moderate - 2.3mi
  3. Silver Dollar Lake and Murray Lake Trail - Moderate - 4.3mi
  4. Island Lake and Ice Lake via Ice Lakes Trail - Hard - 7.5mi
  5. Lower, Middle and Upper Blue Lakes Trail - Hard - 8.7mi
